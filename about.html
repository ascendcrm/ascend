JOSHUA MUNOZ

Founder of Ascend CRM | 10+ Years in Marketing & Growth

For more than 10 years, Joshua has helped service-based businesses generate leads, improve their marketing, and build systems that turn more opportunities into revenue.

Today, he's bringing that experience directly to the home services industry.

After years of working in marketing, Joshua saw how often great contractors were losing business — not because they weren't good at what they did, but because their marketing and follow-up systems weren't keeping up with the quality of their work.

That's why Joshua created Ascend CRM.

Ascend combines proven marketing strategy, automation, AI, and follow-up systems to help home service businesses get more value from every lead and every customer they already have.

Joshua's passion is simple: bring enterprise-level marketing systems to hardworking home service businesses in a way that's practical, measurable, and actually impacts the bottom line.

Because sometimes the biggest opportunity isn't getting more leads.

It's making sure you're maximizing the opportunities already in front of you.
